# Task-Tuesday
An interactive data analysis practice challenge. Explore the dataset, work through the business task, uncover insights, and put your analytical skills to the test.

💡 A Few Hints Before You Start

1. Understand the task first: Read the manager's email carefully. Identify what questions you're expected to answer before jumping into the dataset.

2. Know your dataset: Understand what each column represents and how it relates to the business questions.

3. Clean before analysing: Check for missing values, inconsistent date formats and entries that may need standardisation. Don't assume your dataset is analysis-ready!

4. Watch your dates: Pay attention to the order dates when comparing July, August and September. Could the timing of recorded orders affect your conclusions?

5. Don't look at sales alone: Consider discounts, returns, product categories and customer types when investigating the sales decline.

6. Compare regions: Is the West region experiencing something unique, or is the same pattern happening elsewhere?

7. Tell a story with your findings: Your charts should help explain what happened, why it happened and what the business should consider doing next.

There is no single tool you must use. Excel, Power BI, Tableau, SQL or Python are all welcome.
Focus on your approach, the insights you uncover and how well you communicate your findings.

Happy analysing! 📊
